Medclu · iOS and Android · com.medclu.app

Privacy policy

Last updated 5 October 2026

Medclu keeps your health history on your phone. There is no account and no Medclu server, so there is nothing of yours for us to hold, read, or sell.

This is the public copy of the policy shown inside the app. It describes what the app actually does.

Who we are

Medclu is a private health notebook for one person, published for iPhone and Android. The developer can be reached at eeshbaev@outlook.com. We do not operate an account system or a server that receives your record.

What Medclu stores

Your name, birthday, sex, sport, and height and weight if you add them. Your health issues, daily check-ins, appointments, doctors, medicines and dose log, tests and results, notes, and any photos, scans, or documents you add. All of it is kept in Medclu’s private storage on this phone.

What leaves your phone

Nothing, unless you send it. A backup file, a doctor brief (PDF), or an attached document leaves the phone only when you save or share it, and it goes only where you send it. Medclu does not encrypt backup files, so keep them somewhere you trust.

We do not receive those files. Whoever you send them to — a doctor, a cloud drive, a messaging app — handles them under their own terms.

Permissions, asked when needed

Notifications for your reminders. By default a reminder only says “You have a reminder”; names appear on the lock screen only if you turn that on in Profile. Camera for scans and photos. Your photos or files when you attach one. Face ID, fingerprint, or passcode for the optional app lock. Medclu never receives your biometric data. The phone’s operating system checks it and tells the app only whether the lock opened.

No tracking

No ads. No analytics. No advertising or tracking tools. Medclu does not sell or share your information with anyone. The app does not include third-party analytics, advertising, or crash-reporting services.

Your control

Edit or delete any record at any time. “Delete all my data” in Profile erases everything from this phone. Removing the app removes its data too. Backups and PDFs you saved or sent elsewhere are not affected. Your phone’s own backup service (iCloud or Google) may include app data, depending on your phone’s settings.

Because we never receive the record, we cannot access, correct, or delete it for you. You do that on the phone.

Children

Medclu is not directed at children and does not create accounts. It does not send a child’s information to us. A parent or carer may keep a record on their own phone. That record stays on that phone under the same rules as any other record.

Not medical advice

Medclu records what you and your doctors tell you. It does not interpret results, rate them, or give medical advice. Test values are kept exactly as printed. For any health concern, talk to a doctor.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version and its date will be shown on this page and inside the app.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Write to eeshbaev@outlook.com.

This page does not use cookies, accounts, or analytics.
