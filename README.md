# Medclu privacy policy

Public privacy policy for the Medclu app (iOS and Android, `com.medclu.app`).

The page you can link from the App Store and Google Play is published with GitHub Pages from `index.html`. The text matches the policy shown inside the app.

**Last updated:** 5 October 2026

**Contact:** [eeshbaev@outlook.com](mailto:eeshbaev@outlook.com)

Medclu keeps a person’s health history on their phone. There is no account and no Medclu server. The app does not use ads, analytics, or tracking tools.
